Beta — how to run it, and the report template

Decided on 2026-10-05, after the monetization study (https://claude.ai/artifact/VKFJxVm1PzAY5cc8EHULD1):

  • 40 testers in 3 cohorts, over 6 weeks;
  • a founder price of 30 % off for life for testers;
  • everything is prepared, but the beta is not launched yet.
Cohort Testers Usage profile (invite preset) Who
A 12 Just training (training_only) Gym people who want a routine and nothing else
B 18 Balanced (balanced) Losing fat or recomposing, without obsessing
C 10 Demanding (demanding) Already count macros (MyFitnessPal / MacroFactor users)

Before week 0 (one time)

  1. Production migrations: apply 0029_billing with npm run db:dry-run, then db:apply -- --production (or the admin banner).

  2. Survey: create one Tally form with hidden fields week and cohort, and set BETA_SURVEY_URL on Netlify. The card on Today adds ?week=N&cohort=X in weeks 2, 4 and 6.

  3. Optional: set up Stripe in test mode (below) if testers should see real plans at the end. Without keys, plans say "coming soon" and nobody is charged.

  4. Invite codes: create them in Admin → Beta, one per cohort, with uses = the cohort size and "Founder price" on, for example:

    • A: 12 uses;
    • B: 18 uses;
    • C: 10 uses.

    The link is https://<domain>/register?invite=MAI-B-XXXX. The code sets the cohort, the founder price and the starting usage profile (testers can change it in Profile).

    Signing up with Google or Apple keeps the code too: it travels through the sign-in in the signed OAuth state and is redeemed when the account is created.

Week Do Watch in Admin → Beta

Admin → Beta definitions:

  • Activated: a program and a logged session within 72 h of signing up.
  • DN retention: of people who joined at least N days ago, the share active N or more days after joining. "Active" means any app event or logged session.
  • AI cost: from usage_events, over the last 30 days.
  • NPS: each person's latest answer. NPS = % of 9–10 answers minus % of 0–6 answers.

Meal photos (added 2026-10-15)

Asked for in beta feedback: a photo of the plate → foods, amounts and calories, checked before saving.

  • Cost: one call with a 1280 px photo (≈1,600 image tokens) plus the food list (≈5k tokens, cached) and ≈300 tokens out. On Sonnet 5.5 ≈ 0,8–1,2 cents a photo (≈2 cents on a cache miss); on Haiku ≈ 0,5 cents (FOOD_PHOTO_MODEL). Check Admin → Analytics → "Meal photos".
  • Plans: not on free or Entrena (no food). Coach 10 a month (≈0,10 € worst case on 9,99 €), Coach+ 80 (≈0,80 € on 16,99 €). With ~60 main meals a month, 10 covers the off-plan ones for most people and is meant to leave some wanting more: the reason to move to Coach+. Prices unchanged. The trial gets Coach+'s 80, and so does everyone during the beta (payments off; FOOD_PHOTO_BETA_LIMIT). Phones and tablets only.
  • Watch in the beta: photos per active person a week, the share of reads with confidence: low and of items changed in the review (accuracy), and photos vs words (product event food_photo). If most Coach-level testers need more than 10, decide before payments open whether that is the upsell working or a plan that feels stingy.
  • Privacy: the photo goes to Anthropic like the coach's messages and is not stored. The privacy policy (section 2, 4, 5) and the terms (section 2, 3) say so since LEGAL_VERSION 2026-10-15.

Payments setup (owner's steps)

  1. Create the Stripe account (business details, bank account), and turn on Stripe Tax with the Spanish VAT registration.

  2. Register for the VAT One-Stop Shop (OSS) with the Agencia Tributaria (modelo 035) to sell to consumers in other EU countries. Stripe Tax then charges each country's VAT.

  3. Products and prices: in test mode, run STRIPE_SECRET_KEY=sk_test_… npm run stripe:setup. It creates the 3 products, the 6 tax-inclusive prices and the FOUNDER30 coupon, and prints the env lines.

  4. Netlify env: STRIPE_SECRET_KEY, the six STRIPE_PRICE_* values and STRIPE_COUPON_FOUNDER.

  5. Webhook: in Stripe → Developers → Webhooks, add the endpoint https://<domain>/api/billing/webhook with these events:

    • checkout.session.completed
    • customer.subscription.created, customer.subscription.updated, customer.subscription.deleted
    • invoice.payment_failed

    Then copy the signing secret into STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET.

  6. Customer Portal: in Stripe → Settings → Billing → Customer portal, allow cancelling (at the end of the period), switching between the 3 plans and both intervals, and updating the card.

  7. Test it end to end with the card 4242 4242 4242 4242. When it works, repeat steps 3–6 in live mode (npm run stripe:setup -- --live with the live key).

  8. Legal: have a lawyer review the terms (renewal, cancellation, the 14-day withdrawal right) before charging real money.

  9. Stripe customer emails: in Stripe → Settings → Customer emails, turn on successful payment receipts, failed payment emails and the upcoming renewal reminder (yearly plans). The app sends its own messages for the trial, plan changes, cancellation and the end of a plan (lib/notify), and shows a banner to update the card while a payment is past due.

Admin digest and alerts (optional keys)

Every Monday each admin gets a digest by email (AI cost, people, coach errors, database size, version, payments). Alerts arrive at once, by email and push, at most once per kind per day. Optional Netlify env vars:

  • ADMIN_AI_BUDGET_EUR: monthly AI budget for the cost alerts.
  • ADMIN_DB_LIMIT_GB (default 8): the database limit for the 80 % alert.
  • ANTHROPIC_ADMIN_KEY: org-wide Anthropic spend in the digest.
  • NETLIFY_API_TOKEN + NETLIFY_SITE_ID: the latest deploy, and an alert when it failed.
