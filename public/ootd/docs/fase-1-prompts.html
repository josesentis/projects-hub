OOTD — Freepik image prompts

Prompts to generate the photography for the landing page with Freepik's AI Image Generator (or any text-to-image tool). All images should feel like one cohesive fashion campaign — the grade, background and lighting stay constant, but the people and the styles are deliberately diverse.

Global rules (apply to EVERY image)

  • Format: vertical 3:4, high resolution.
  • Look: editorial fashion photography, photorealistic (NOT illustration / 3D render).
  • Background: plain dark charcoal / neutral seamless studio (so subjects cut out cleanly and the dark UI stays consistent).
  • Lighting: soft, directional, cinematic studio light.
  • Finish: subtle film grain, consistent color grade across the whole set.

Suffix to paste at the end of every prompt:

plain dark charcoal seamless studio background, soft directional studio lighting, cinematic editorial fashion photography, subtle film grain, photorealistic, natural skin texture, vertical 3:4

🌍 Diversity rules (IMPORTANT — read before generating)

OOTD styles everyone, so the cast must look like everyone:

  • Ethnicity: spread across the set — Black, East Asian, South Asian, Latino/Hispanic, Middle Eastern, White, mixed-race. Don't repeat the same look twice in a row.
  • Gender: mix women, men and androgynous/non-binary presentation roughly evenly.
  • Age & body: include 20s through 50s+, and a range of body types (slim, athletic, mid-size, plus-size), plus varied heights and natural hair (curly, afro, buzzed, long, grey, hijab, etc.).
  • Style register: deliberately mix sporty / athleisure, smart-casual, tailored/formal, streetwear, minimal, bohemian, utilitarian across the page — not all "elegant editorial".
  • Each prompt below already names a person + a style register. Treat them as a starting point — swap freely so your final set feels varied. The only things to keep constant are background, lighting and grade.

💡 Tip: Freepik sometimes defaults to one body/ethnicity — state it explicitly ("a [age] [ethnicity] [gender] model with [hair/body detail]") or it'll drift. Generate 2–3 variants per slot and pick the one that adds the most variety to the set.

How to place them

Drop each generated image into the matching slot in the prototype (OOTD.html) — the slots persist your drop — or, in the production build, wire them as the src of the corresponding <img>/next/image per the handoff README. Match counts: 7 hero · 5 today's looks · 6 styles · 8 lookbook.

Cohesion vs. variety: the people and styles should vary a lot; the background, lighting and color grade must stay identical across all 26 images so the page still reads as one campaign. If a generated image has a different background or grade, regenerate it rather than keeping it.
