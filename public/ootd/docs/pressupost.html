OOTD — Launch budget (initial one-time investment)

A concrete kickoff "Launch Kit" — the one-time spend to take OOTD from a working V1 to a real launch (brand + go-to-market + validation). This is not ongoing startup financials, and it does not include the founder's development salary (that's a separate runway line — add it per the months you need).

Figures are orientative freelance/agency ranges for Barcelona/Spain. Not financial advice — confirm with a gestoría and a lawyer.

Two versions

🟢 LEAN launch — ~€25,000

Freelance branding, ~€1.5k/mo ads, UGC instead of expensive production, semi-moderated tests. Enough to validate and launch with dignity.

🔵 SOLID launch — ~€45,000–50,000

Small-studio branding, ~€2.5k/mo ads + pro management, stronger content production, 40 fully moderated tests with a report, complete legal. Recommended if the angel cheque is sizeable and you want a launch people notice.

Notes

  • Founder salary is NOT in here. This is the launch kit (brand + go-to-market + validation). Add your dev salary separately based on the runway months you need.
  • Spend on product + retention first, paid growth second. Paid acquisition before product-market fit burns cash filling a leaky bucket. The 3-month paid-media line is for learning (which channels/creatives work), not for scaling.
  • Don't hire standing teams yet. Branding and UX here are one-off freelance/agency work, not permanent headcount. Full marketing/branding/social teams are a Series-A profile, post-PMF.
  • Size the raise to a milestone. e.g. "with €X I reach Y active users and Z% 30-day retention in 9–12 months" — that's what unlocks the next, larger round.
  • Formalise the friend's cheque (SAFE or convertible note, clear terms, in writing, with a lawyer) even though they're a friend.

What "App Store assets / ASO" means

The visuals + copy on your App Store / Google Play listing — what people see before downloading: app icon, designed screenshots (with short captions), a 15–30s preview video, and the title / subtitle / description / keywords. ASO (App Store Optimization) = SEO for the stores: the screenshots are designed to sell, and keywords make you findable for "outfit", "what to wear", "AI stylist". It's your shop window — good listing conversion makes every ad euro go further.
